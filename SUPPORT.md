# Support — Modern Calculator

## Need help?

- **Bug reports and feature requests:** [bugs.rdcapps.com](https://bugs.rdcapps.com)
- **Email:** [support@rdcapps.com](mailto:support@rdcapps.com)
- **Documentation and issues:** [GitHub repository](https://github.com/deniscasian699/Modern-Calculator)

Please include the app version, Android version, device model, a short description of the issue and, when helpful, screenshots or steps to reproduce it. Do not include private calculations, payment details or other sensitive information.

## Common help topics

### Restore purchases

Open **Settings → Support** and choose **Restore Purchase**. Use the same Google Play account that completed the purchase. Restoration requires an internet connection. Premium does not remove ads; Remove Ads or an active Supporter subscription does.

### Change ad privacy choices

Open **Settings → Support → Privacy Settings** to open the available privacy choices again. Availability depends on your region and Google’s consent flow.

### Clear or reuse history

History is kept locally on your device. Open History to reuse a result, or choose **Clear History** to remove stored entries. The default limit is 100 entries; Premium unlocks up to 500. Remove Ads alone does not raise the limit.

### Currency rates

Currency conversion needs an internet connection to refresh exchange rates. Other core calculator functions and most tools work locally.

## Response times

We aim to reply within five business days. Privacy requests are handled within the period required by applicable law.
